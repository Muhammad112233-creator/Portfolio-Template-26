I am Adrian Voss, an independent art director. Driven by detail, I design immersive experiences for brands, games and live events.

Never Ordinary

I build immersive worlds made to be remembered. My 360° practice spans art direction, 3D craft, motion and interaction — helping teams find a bold, honest and timeless identity.

For more than twelve years I have worked across the digital field — designing worlds where design, 3D, motion and sound meet.

Much of that path ran through the luxury industry, where nothing survives without detail. I keep one eye on new tools and trends, and the other on what never changes — craftsmanship.

Today I focus on games and real-time worlds, chasing images and places people haven't seen before — uncharted territory, on purpose.
